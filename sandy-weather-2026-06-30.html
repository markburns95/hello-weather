A warm and cloudless morning greets Sandy residents as June draws to a close. Temperatures climb steadily from a comfortable 64°F at 5 am to a hot 89°F by 10 am. No precipitation is expected; the Wasatch Valley remains firmly in its summer dry pattern. Outdoor activities are best completed early.

Forecast Summary

The morning of June 30 follows the established pattern for the Wasatch Front this week: overnight lows in the low-to-mid 60s giving way to rapidly rising daytime temperatures. Expect full sunshine, near-zero humidity, and UV index values in the “Very High” range by mid-morning. Sandy sits at approximately 4,500 ft elevation; sunscreen is advised even before 9 am.

Hikers heading to the canyons above Sandy should plan to be back at the trailhead by 10 am at the latest — temperatures above 9,000 ft will still be pleasant, but the valley floor is already warming quickly.
